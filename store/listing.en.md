# DRUM LAB: Drum Tuner — draft Google Play listing (English)
**Title:** DRUM LAB: Drum Tuner
**Short description:** Tune drums in Hz, compare lugs and save tuning presets on your phone.

**Full description:**
DRUM LAB is a drum tuning assistant that listens to drum hits through your phone microphone and estimates frequencies in Hz.

• Compare frequency measurements near individual tension rods.
• Tune batter and resonant heads separately; check the center strike.
• View the nearest musical note and explore possible spectral peaks.
• Start with Contemporary Worship presets for toms, kick and snare.
• Customize drums and save your measurements locally.
• Export and import backups as JSON.
• Offline functionality. No ads or account required in this release.

The detected spectral peak may be an overtone rather than the drum's fundamental pitch. Recheck readings and confirm tuning by ear. The microphone is used only for frequency analysis on your device. No audio is uploaded.
